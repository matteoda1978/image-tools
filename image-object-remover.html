AI results are not always perfect. Detailed textures, large objects, and complicated backgrounds may need several attempts with smaller selections.

Tips for cleaner results

Cover the whole object

Include the edges and any visible shadow. A selection that is too small can leave a halo behind.

Work in small sections

For large or complex objects, try separate smaller strokes instead of masking a huge area all at once.

Keep some context

Leave nearby background visible around the selection so the AI has surrounding details to work from.
