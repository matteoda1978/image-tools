Tips for cleaner object removal

Cover the whole object

Include the edges and any visible shadow. A selection that is too small can leave parts of the object or a visible outline behind.

Work in small sections

For large or complex objects, try separate smaller strokes instead of masking a huge area all at once.

Keep some context

Leave nearby background visible around the selection so the AI has surrounding details to work from.

How to remove an object from a photo

An unwanted object can distract from the main subject of a photograph. AI object removal, also known as image inpainting, attempts to fill the selected area using the surrounding parts of the image. RitoccaPix uses an AI inpainting model to reconstruct the selected area directly in your browser.

  1. Upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP photograph.
  2. Adjust the brush size to match the object you want to remove.
  3. Paint over the object and its visible edges.
  4. Select Remove object with AI.
  5. Compare the edited image with the original photograph.
  6. Download the result as a PNG file.

You can undo brush strokes before processing. If the reconstruction is not satisfactory, return to your selection and try a smaller or more precise mask.

What can you remove from a photo?

The AI object remover can help remove unwanted elements and distractions from photographs, including:

  • People or passers-by in the background.
  • Small objects that distract from the main subject.
  • Cables, wires, and other unwanted details.
  • Signs and visual clutter in the background.
  • Small items, marks, or objects that interrupt a photograph.

Results depend on the size of the selected area and the details behind it. Removing a small object against a simple background is generally easier than reconstructing a large area with complex textures or important details.

The tool is designed for general photo cleanup. It cannot guarantee perfect restoration in every image.
