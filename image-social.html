Free Social Media Image Maker

RitoccaPix Social Media Studio helps you prepare images for social platforms without installing design software. Choose a platform preset, add your photo, adjust its position and zoom, then add a headline or short caption. Download the finished graphic as a PNG or JPG file.

Supported social media image sizes

  • Instagram: square feed posts, portrait posts and vertical Stories or Reels.
  • Facebook: landscape posts and wide cover images.
  • Pinterest: vertical Pins.
  • LinkedIn: landscape feed posts.
  • X: landscape posts.
  • YouTube: 16:9 video thumbnails.

Platform specifications can change and placements may crop images differently across devices. Keep important text away from the edges and review the preview before downloading.

How to create a social media graphic

  1. Choose the platform format you need.
  2. Select a photo from your device.
  3. Drag the photo in the preview to adjust the crop, or change zoom and placement settings.
  4. Add a headline and optional caption, then choose text and background colors.
  5. Download your finished design as PNG or JPG.

Frequently asked questions

Is this social media image maker free?

Yes. You can create and download designs without creating an account. There is no watermark added by the tool.

Which social platforms are supported?

The preset list includes image sizes for Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, LinkedIn, X and YouTube. You can switch formats before downloading.

Can I move the photo inside the design?

Yes. After selecting a photo, drag it directly in the preview to adjust its position. Use the zoom slider to change the crop.

Are my photos uploaded?

No image upload to a RitoccaPix processing server is performed by this editor. Your browser loads the website and any resources needed to display it, while your selected image is processed locally on your device.

Should I download PNG or JPG?

PNG is useful for crisp graphics and text. JPG often creates a smaller file for photographs. JPG does not preserve transparency.
