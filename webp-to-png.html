RitoccaPix converts one or multiple WebP images to PNG directly in your browser. PNG supports transparency, so transparent areas in the original WebP can remain transparent in the converted image. Preview your results and download individual PNG files or a ZIP archive. Your selected image files are processed locally on your device.

Why convert WebP to PNG?

WebP is a modern image format commonly used on websites to reduce file sizes. Some apps, editing workflows and online services require PNG files. Converting WebP to PNG can make an image easier to use in those situations, especially when transparency matters.

Does PNG preserve transparency?

Yes. PNG supports transparency. This converter uses a transparent canvas, allowing transparent areas in the original image to remain transparent in the PNG output.

Will the converted PNG look exactly the same?

The converter keeps the original image dimensions. PNG uses lossless compression, but a WebP image that was originally saved with lossy compression cannot regain detail that was already lost.

Are my images uploaded?

The conversion is performed locally in your browser. The selected image files are not intentionally uploaded to RitoccaPix servers. The page loads an external ZIP library only when needed for batch downloads.
