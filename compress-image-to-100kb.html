Why Compress Images to 100KB?

A smaller image can be easier to upload to websites, online forms, email services and applications that limit file size. The right target depends on the upload requirements and the image itself.

This free image compressor works in your browser. Your selected image is not uploaded to a RitoccaPix image-processing server. Very small targets may require reducing image dimensions or quality, which can affect visual detail.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress an image to 100KB online?

Upload an image, select 100KB and click Compress Image. Download the result when processing finishes.

Will every image become smaller than 100KB?

The tool tries to reach the selected target by adjusting image quality and, if necessary, image dimensions. Some images may not reach the target without substantial quality loss.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The image processing takes place locally in your browser. Your selected image is not sent to a RitoccaPix processing server.

Can I compress PNG images?

Yes. You can open PNG files and export them as JPG or WebP. These formats do not preserve PNG transparency in the same way, so check the result before using it.

Is this image compressor free?

Yes. You can use this tool for free in your browser without installing an application.
