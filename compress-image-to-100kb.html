Compress PNG to 100KB

PNG images can be larger than JPG files, especially when they contain detailed graphics or large dimensions. You can upload a PNG and convert it to JPG or WebP to create a smaller file.

Remember that converting a transparent PNG to JPG removes transparency. JPG output uses a white background. WebP may support transparency, but check the result and make sure the destination accepts that format.

JPG vs WebP: Which Output Format Is Better?

JPG

JPG is widely supported by browsers, websites, document portals and online forms. It is a practical option when compatibility matters. JPG does not preserve transparent backgrounds.

WebP

WebP is a modern image format designed for efficient image compression. It can be a useful choice for websites, but not every upload service accepts WebP files. Check the format requirements before submitting.
