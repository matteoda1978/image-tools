How to compress an image online

  1. Select an image from your device.
  2. Choose JPG, WebP or PNG as the output format.
  3. Adjust the compression quality.
  4. Click “Compress image”.
  5. Preview the result and download your compressed image.

Why compress images?

Smaller image files can help reduce storage use, make sharing easier and improve website loading times. The best quality setting depends on the image and how you plan to use it.

JPG and WebP support adjustable lossy compression. PNG is a lossless format, so the quality slider does not control its compression in the same way.

Frequently asked questions

Is RitoccaPix free?

Yes. This image compressor is free to use.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Image processing takes place locally in your browser.

Which formats are supported?

You can select JPG, WebP or PNG as the output format. The formats available for decoding depend on your browser.

Will compression always reduce the file size?

No. The result depends on the original image, output format and quality setting. A converted file can sometimes be larger than the original.
