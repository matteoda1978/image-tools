How to Resize 50 Images at Once

  1. Select multiple JPG, JPEG, PNG or WebP images from your device.
  2. Enter the width and/or height you want for the output images.
  3. Keep proportions enabled to avoid stretching the photos.
  4. Choose the quality and output format for the whole batch.
  5. Click Process all images and wait for the results.
  6. Download individual images or use the ZIP download to save them together.

Compress JPG, PNG and WebP Images

JPG is commonly used for photographs, PNG is useful for graphics and transparency, and WebP can produce smaller files for many web images. For JPG and WebP, lowering the quality setting can reduce file size, although it may reduce visible detail. PNG output is lossless, but converting an image to PNG does not necessarily make it smaller.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I resize 50 photos at once?

Yes. You can select up to 50 supported images and apply the same width, height, quality and output format settings to the entire batch.

Can I add more photos after selecting some?

Yes. Select or drag additional images into the upload area. Previously selected images remain in the list, and you can remove individual images before processing.

Can I keep the original proportions?

Yes. Keep the proportions option enabled to fit each image inside the dimensions you enter without stretching it. Disable this option only when you want to apply exact width and height values.

Can I download all processed photos together?

Yes. Use the ZIP download button to collect the processed images in one archive. You can also download each image separately.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image processing takes place locally in your browser. Your selected image files are not sent to our server by this tool.
