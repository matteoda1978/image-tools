How to create your social video

  1. Upload your photos and arrange their order.
  2. Choose a vertical, square or landscape format.
  3. Add a title and select the duration of each photo.
  4. Choose the video resolution.
  5. Create and download your video.
  6. To add music, upload the video to TikTok or Instagram and choose an available track.

This version exports WebM when supported by your browser. MP4 conversion is not included.
