How to create your social video

  1. Upload and arrange your photos.
  2. Choose a vertical, square or landscape format.
  3. Add a title and select the duration of each photo.
  4. Find music from one of the linked services, then upload an audio file you can use.
  5. Set the volume and fade effects.
  6. Create and download your video.

This version exports WebM when supported by your browser. MP4 conversion is not included in this version.
