How to create a video from photos

  1. Upload one or more photos from your device.
  2. Choose vertical, square, or landscape format.
  3. Add a title and set the duration for each image.
  4. Choose HD or Full HD resolution.
  5. Click “Create video” and wait for it to finish.
  6. Click the green link to download the WebM video.

If your phone is slow, try HD resolution first. The video is exported as WebM; MP4 export is not currently available.

Photo to video maker: frequently asked questions

Can I make a video for TikTok, Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Choose the vertical 9:16 format for short-form social videos. You can also create square or landscape videos.

Can I add music to my video?

This tool does not currently add an audio track. Download your video first, then add music using the relevant platform's music library or a video editor.

Are my photos uploaded to RitoccaPix?

The tool processes your selected photos locally in your browser. It does not upload them to a RitoccaPix server.

Which download format is supported?

WebM is currently supported. MP4 export is not available in this version.
