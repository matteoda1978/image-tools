Free gaming avatar and profile picture maker

RitoccaPix Gaming Avatar & Profile Picture Maker is a browser-based editor for creating original gaming graphics for many games and communities. Upload your own photo, character screenshot, team logo or creator image; crop it, add a gamer name or subtitle, choose a frame and export a design for a profile picture, video thumbnail, banner, vertical story or portrait post. If you prefer illustrated characters, switch to Pixel Avatar mode to build a custom pixel-art character from scratch.

How to make a gaming graphic

  1. Choose Photo & graphics to upload an image, or select Pixel avatar to create a pixel-art character.
  2. Choose the output format that fits your intended use.
  3. Adjust image zoom and position. Add a gamer name, subtitle, text color and frame if you want them.
  4. Preview the result and select Download PNG. Photo editing and pixel-art drawing happen in your browser.

Made for different games and platforms

The editor is not limited to a specific game. Use your own suitable images to create profile graphics for gaming communities, creator channels, esports teams and social accounts. Available formats include square profile pictures, 16:9 video thumbnails, 3:1 banners, vertical 9:16 graphics and 4:5 posts. Check the destination platform’s current image rules and crop requirements before publishing. RitoccaPix is not affiliated with or endorsed by game publishers or social platforms.

Frequently asked questions

Is this avatar maker free?

Yes. You can customize, save and download your avatar without creating an account or adding a watermark.

Are my designs uploaded?

No. Character drawing and saved favorites stay in your browser. A share link contains your design settings in the URL; it does not upload an image.

Can I use the avatar on Discord or gaming profiles?

You can use the downloaded PNG wherever the platform's profile-image rules allow. Check the platform's terms and community guidelines.

Does this create official game skins or 3D characters?

No. It creates original 2D graphics and pixel avatars. It does not create official skins or modify game files.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Image composition happens locally in your browser. The page does not upload the selected image to a RitoccaPix server.
