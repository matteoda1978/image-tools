Good to know

Designed to be clear and transparent

Can I use scanned textbook pages?

Yes. Photo OCR and scanned-page recognition can read text, but image quality affects accuracy. Review the extracted text before creating your study pack.

Does it already use generative AI?

No. The current version provides local text extraction and source-based study aids. The conversational AI tutor will be added only after its secure connection is ready.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The current text extraction process runs in your browser. External libraries are loaded to enable PDF reading and OCR.

Can I download my study pack?

Yes. Once a study pack has been created, you can download its contents as a text file for later revision.
