Frequently asked questions

About this preview

Can I upload a PDF here now?

Not yet. This page presents the planned product and an interactive sample. Document upload and AI processing will need to be implemented and tested separately.

Will it work for different school levels?

The intended design includes middle school, high school, university and independent learning. The explanations and question difficulty should adapt to the selected level.

Will the study pack always be correct?

No AI system can guarantee that every answer is correct. A working version should show source references where possible and encourage students to verify important facts against their course material.

Will my documents be private?

Privacy and document handling must be designed before uploads are enabled. This preview does not upload documents or send the text in the example field to a server.
