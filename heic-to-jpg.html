Convert HEIC to JPG online

HEIC is an image format commonly used by modern smartphones, including iPhones. JPG is widely supported by devices, software, websites, and online services.

HEIC and HEIF images are decoded with the HEIC conversion engine. JPG, PNG, and WebP images are processed directly by your browser without using the HEIC decoder.

Select one image or multiple images, adjust the JPG quality, choose a maximum image dimension if needed, and click Convert to JPG. When you convert multiple images, the results are packaged into a ZIP file.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert HEIC to JPG?

Select your HEIC or HEIF images, choose your preferred quality and size, then click Convert to JPG. The converted file will be downloaded to your device.

Can I convert multiple HEIC images at once?

Yes. Select multiple images and convert them together. The resulting JPG files will be packaged into a ZIP archive.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

This tool processes images in your browser. HEIC decoding and conversion take place on your device using the included browser-side libraries.

Can I reduce the image size?

Yes. Choose a maximum dimension of 4096, 3000, or 2000 pixels. The original aspect ratio is preserved.
