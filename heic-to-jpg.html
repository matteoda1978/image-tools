Why convert HEIC to JPG?

HEIC and HEIF are image formats commonly used by Apple devices. They can store high-quality photos efficiently, but some websites, apps and older devices do not support them.

JPG is widely supported by browsers, computers, smartphones, social networks and online forms. Converting HEIC to JPG can make photos easier to share, upload and open on different devices.

Does JPG quality affect file size?

Yes. A higher quality setting generally preserves more visual detail but produces a larger file. A lower setting can reduce file size, although excessive compression may create visible artefacts.

Can I convert HEIC to JPG on Windows?

Can I reduce the dimensions of my photos?

Yes. Choose a maximum dimension from the size menu. Images larger than that limit are scaled down while preserving their proportions.

Will converting HEIC to JPG reduce image quality?

JPG uses lossy compression, so some image data may be lost. A higher quality setting can help preserve visual detail, but cannot guarantee an identical copy of the original.

Does JPG support transparency?

No. JPG does not support transparent pixels. HEIC photos are usually photographs, but any transparent areas in an image would need a background when saved as JPG.

Why did my HEIC file fail to convert?

The file may be damaged, use an unsupported encoding, or be too large for the available browser memory. Try converting fewer files at once or using a smaller source image.

Can I use this converter on a phone?

Yes, on compatible mobile browsers. Very large photos or batches may take longer and may exceed the memory available on some phones.
