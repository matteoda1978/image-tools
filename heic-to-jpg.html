Convert HEIC to JPG online

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is an image format commonly used by Apple devices, including iPhones. It can store high-quality photos in relatively small files.

JPG is one of the most widely supported image formats. Converting HEIC to JPG can make photos easier to open, share, upload to websites and use with software that does not support HEIC.

How to use this HEIC converter

  • Select one or more HEIC or HEIF images from your device.
  • Choose the JPG quality you want.
  • Optionally select a maximum image dimension.
  • Click Convert to JPG.
  • Download the JPG image or the ZIP archive containing your converted files.

When you select multiple images, the converter places the successful conversions into a single ZIP archive. Files that cannot be converted are reported in the status message.

Image quality and resizing

The JPG quality setting controls the compression level of the resulting images. A higher quality setting generally produces larger files, while a lower setting can reduce file size.

You can keep the original image dimensions or choose a maximum dimension of 4096, 3000 or 2000 pixels. When resizing is needed, the original aspect ratio is preserved.

Privacy and image processing

The selected image files are processed locally in your browser. This page does not send your selected photos to RitoccaPix servers. The HEIC conversion and ZIP libraries are loaded from jsDelivr, a third-party content delivery network. Your browser needs an internet connection to load these libraries.

The converted JPG files are created using browser image-processing features. Original EXIF information, such as camera details, GPS coordinates and capture dates, is not fully preserved. Keep your original photos if you need that information.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert HEIC to JPG?

Select your HEIC or HEIF images, choose your preferred JPG quality and image dimensions, then click Convert to JPG. The converted file will be downloaded to your device.

What is the difference between HEIC and JPG?

HEIC is a modern image format designed to store photos efficiently. JPG is an older and widely compatible format supported by most devices, browsers, applications and websites.

Can I convert multiple HEIC images at once?

Yes. You can select up to 50 images in one batch. Successfully converted images are packaged into a ZIP archive when more than one file is selected.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The selected images are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to RitoccaPix servers. The conversion libraries are downloaded from jsDelivr, an external content delivery network.

Can I reduce the image dimensions?

Yes. You can keep the original dimensions or choose a maximum dimension of 4096, 3000 or 2000 pixels. The original aspect ratio is preserved.

Is the original photo metadata preserved?

No. JPG files are created through the browser canvas, which does not preserve all original EXIF metadata. Keep your original photos if you need the original capture date, GPS data or camera information.

Why can't I convert some HEIC files?

Some files may use unsupported encoding features, may be damaged, or may exceed the processing limits of your browser. Try another original image and make sure your browser can load the conversion libraries.

Is this HEIC to JPG converter free?

Yes. You can use this converter online for free without creating an account or registering.
