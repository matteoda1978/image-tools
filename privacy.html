Many RitoccaPix tools are designed to process selected images directly in your browser, without uploading the image to a RitoccaPix-operated image-processing server.

Processing methods may differ between tools. Some features may load software libraries, services or AI models from third-party providers. Those requests may disclose technical information to the relevant provider.

Check the instructions displayed on each tool page to understand how that feature works. Do not use a tool for confidential material unless you have verified its processing method and the services it uses.

For more information, visit Google's Advertising Privacy and Terms .

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to personal information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, objection or data portability. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
