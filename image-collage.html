How to create a creative photo collage

  1. Upload up to 12 photos from your device.
  2. Choose Create a Local Smart Collage for a private, browser-only layout, or select one of the five AI Smart Collage styles.
  3. For AI, choose Big & Small, Equal Photos, Overlapping, Creative Mix or Mosaic. Enter an optional design direction, tick the consent box and click Create AI Smart Collage.
  4. Choose an enhancement style and apply it to your collage photos.
  5. Choose a creative frame shape, then adjust the canvas format, frame spacing, background and caption.
  6. Download your finished collage as a high-resolution JPG or PNG.

Local Smart Collage and final collage rendering run in your browser. If you choose AI Smart Collage, reduced-size photo previews are sent to our cloud AI service for layout analysis after you give consent. Original image files stay in your browser; the returned layout is applied locally.

Frequently asked questions

Does Smart Collage use artificial intelligence?

There are two options. Local Smart Collage uses layout rules and does not use an AI model. AI Smart Collage sends reduced photo previews to our cloud AI service, powered by Google Gemini, to analyse the photos and suggest a layout. It does not generate or replace your photos.

What AI collage styles are available?

You can choose Big & Small, Equal Photos, Overlapping, Creative Mix or Mosaic. Each style gives the AI a different composition direction.

Can AI help avoid cutting off faces?

The editor supports AI-provided focal point information to position important subjects more carefully inside a frame. Results depend on the information returned by the AI service. You can always adjust the photo position and zoom manually.

Can I center, zoom or show the whole photo?

Yes. Select a frame, adjust the zoom slider, drag the photo to reposition it, or use Fill frame, Fit whole photo, Center & reset zoom, or Auto-fit all photos.

Does photo enhancement overwrite my original photos?

No. The original uploaded images remain unchanged. The selected enhancement is applied when the collage is drawn and exported.

Does this tool use a cloud AI service?

Only if you choose AI Smart Collage, tick the consent box and click an AI button. Reduced-size photo previews are sent to the cloud AI service for layout analysis. The local composition option does not use a cloud service.

Are my photos uploaded?

In local mode, photos stay in your browser. If you explicitly choose AI Smart Collage and give consent, reduced-size JPEG previews are uploaded for layout analysis. Original image files are not sent to the AI service. The final collage is assembled in your browser.
