Frequently asked questions

Does Smart Collage use artificial intelligence?

Smart Collage uses layout rules to create varied, magazine-style arrangements; it does not use a generative AI model or send photos to a cloud AI service. Your photos remain on your device.

Can I center, zoom or show the whole photo?

Yes. Select a frame, drag the photo to reposition it, adjust zoom, or rotate the complete photo frame together with its image using the rotation slider or 90-degree buttons. You can also use Fill frame, Fit whole photo, Center & reset zoom, or Auto-fit all photos.

Does photo enhancement overwrite my original photos?

No. The original uploaded images remain unchanged. The selected enhancement is applied when the collage is drawn and exported.

Does this tool use a cloud AI service?

No. Layout generation and photo appearance adjustments run in your browser. The composition feature uses smart layout rules and variation rather than a generative AI model.

Can I use the tool on my phone?

Yes. You can upload photos, choose rounded, rectangular, circular, oval, heart, cloud or star-shaped frames, create layouts, apply enhancement styles and export your collage on supported mobile browsers.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Images are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded by this tool.
