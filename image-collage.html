How to create a photo collage

  1. Choose a ready-made composition or start with a blank canvas.
  2. Add photos from your phone or computer.
  3. Add, move and resize frames to create your own layout.
  4. Adjust the canvas format, spacing, corners and background color.
  5. Download your finished collage as JPG or PNG.

Your photos are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded by this tool.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Photo Collage Maker free?

Yes. You can create and download collages without registration.

Can I design my own layout?

Yes. Start with a blank canvas or choose a template and customize its frames.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. This tool processes selected images locally in your browser.
