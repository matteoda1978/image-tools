Photo Collage Maker

Create a photo collage your way. Start with a blank canvas or a ready-made layout, then place, move, resize and overlap frames. Download your collage as a high-resolution JPG or PNG.

Photos are placed automatically. Select a thumbnail and a frame to edit the composition, or drag a photo inside its frame.

2. Choose a layout

Choosing a layout replaces the current frame arrangement. Photos are automatically assigned to the available frames in order. You can move, resize and overlap frames. On a phone, drag the frame border to move the whole frame, drag the photo center to move the photo inside, and use a corner handle to resize.
