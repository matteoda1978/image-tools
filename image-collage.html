How to make your photo collage

  1. Choose a layout or start with a blank canvas.
  2. Upload your photos. They will be placed automatically in the available frames.
  3. Select a frame and a photo if you want to change the automatic arrangement.
  4. Drag inside a frame to reposition its photo. Drag an edge to move the frame, or a corner handle to resize it.
  5. Adjust the format, spacing, corners, background and caption.
  6. Download your finished collage as a JPG or PNG image.

Photos are processed locally in your browser. This tool does not upload your images to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Can I move a photo inside its frame?

Yes. Drag inside the photo area to reposition it. Use “Center photo” to return it to the center.

Can I make one photo bigger than the others?

Yes. Select a frame and drag any corner handle to resize it independently.

Can I overlap frames?

Yes. Move a frame over another to create your own composition. Frames later in the frame list appear on top.

Can I use my phone?

Yes. You can upload photos, reposition them and resize frames using touch gestures.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Images are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded by this tool.

Is the Photo Collage Maker free?

Yes. You can create and download collages for free without registering.
