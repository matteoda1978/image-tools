How to create a photo collage

  1. Select your photos from your device.
  2. Choose a preset layout or select Custom to create your own.
  3. In Custom mode, add frames and adjust their position, size and zoom.
  4. Customize the canvas format, spacing, corners and background color.
  5. Download your collage in JPG or PNG format.

Your photos are processed directly in your browser. They are not uploaded by this tool to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Photo Collage Maker free?

Yes. You can create and download collages without registration.

Can I create a custom collage?

Yes. Select Custom to add, remove, resize and reposition frames. Each frame can also have its own zoom setting.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. This tool processes your selected photos locally in your browser.
