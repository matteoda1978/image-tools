Image Tools makes it easy to resize photos and graphics directly in your browser. Set the width and height you need, preserve the original aspect ratio, and choose the format that best suits your project. You can process up to 50 images in one batch.

How to resize images

  1. Select one or more images from your device, or drag them into the upload area.
  2. Enter your desired width and height in pixels.
  3. Keep the aspect ratio enabled to avoid stretching your images.
  4. Choose JPG, PNG or WebP and adjust the quality if needed.
  5. Click “Resize all images”. Download files individually or use the ZIP button.

Why resize images?

Smaller dimensions can make images more suitable for websites, online stores, email attachments, presentations and social media. Resizing can also reduce file size, although the result depends on the original image, output format and quality.

Choose the right image format

  • JPG: useful for photographs and images where a compact file is important.
  • PNG: useful for graphics and images that need transparency.
  • WebP: a modern format often suitable for websites and online content.

Frequently asked questions

Is this image resizer free?

Yes. You can resize images online for free without creating an account.

Can I resize multiple images at once?

Yes. Select up to 50 images in one batch and apply the same target dimensions and output settings to all of them.

Can I download all resized images together?

Yes. After resizing, click “Download All as ZIP” to download the successfully processed images in one archive.

Will my images be stretched?

Not when “Keep aspect ratio” is enabled. Each image is scaled to fit within the requested width and height without stretching.

Does resizing reduce image quality?

Reducing dimensions removes pixels, and JPG or WebP compression can affect quality. Use a higher quality setting when preserving detail is important.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Resizing is performed locally in your browser. The ZIP library is loaded from a content delivery network to create the archive.
