Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image resizer free?

Yes. You can resize images online for free without registering or installing software.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The selected image is processed locally in your browser by this tool.

How do I resize an image without distorting it?

Keep the original proportions option enabled. When you change the width or height, the other dimension is calculated automatically.

Can I resize an image to a specific pixel size?

Yes. Enter the desired width and height. If you disable the proportions option, you can set both dimensions independently, but the image may look stretched.

Does resizing reduce image quality?

Reducing dimensions removes pixels and may reduce detail. This tool exports the resized image as a JPEG file, using a high quality setting. JPEG is a lossy format.

Which image formats can I use?

You can select image formats supported by your browser. The resized result is downloaded as a JPEG image.
