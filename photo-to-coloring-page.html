Frequently asked questions

What kind of photo works best?

Use a sharp, well-lit photo with a clear subject and a simple background. Very dark, blurry or crowded pictures may create less distinct lines.

Is the coloring page generated online?

The tool runs in your browser. Your selected photo is processed on your device and is not uploaded to a RitoccaPix server.

Can I print it on A4 paper?

Yes. Select Print A4 and choose A4 paper in your browser's print dialog. The browser controls the final printer margins and scaling.

Will every photo look like a professional illustration?

Results depend on the original photo. This tool detects visible brightness and colour boundaries and filters some small marks. It does not understand objects like a human illustrator, so clear photos with simple backgrounds usually work best.
