Frequently asked questions

What kind of photo works best?

Use a sharp, well-lit photo with a clear subject. Busy backgrounds, shadows and complex textures can still produce extra lines.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The image is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to a RitoccaPix server.

Can I print on A4 paper?

Yes. Select Print A4 and choose A4 paper in your browser's print dialog. Printer margins and scaling depend on your browser and printer.

Will every photo look like a professional illustration?

Results depend on the original image. This tool simplifies colours and detects boundaries; it does not semantically recognize objects or replace a human illustrator.
