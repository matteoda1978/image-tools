Frequently asked questions

How do I get my free credit?

Create an account and verify your email address using the confirmation link sent to your inbox. One free credit is added to your account after your email is verified.

What kind of photo works best?

Use a sharp, well-lit photo with a clear subject and a simple background. Very dark, blurry or crowded pictures may produce less distinct results.

How is my coloring page generated?

The selected photo is sent from your browser to the RitoccaPix processing service hosted on Cloudflare. The service sends it to OpenAI to generate the coloring page and returns the result to your browser.

Is my photo processed locally?

No. This AI feature requires online processing. Your photo is transmitted to Cloudflare and OpenAI. Please read the Privacy Policy before using the feature and do not upload sensitive or confidential images.

Can I print it on A4 paper?

Yes. Select Print A4 and choose A4 paper in your browser's print dialog. The browser controls the final printer margins and scaling.

Will every photo look exactly like a professional illustration?

AI results can vary depending on the original photo, its lighting, subject and background. The tool aims to create clean coloring-book outlines, but results are not guaranteed to be identical for every image.
