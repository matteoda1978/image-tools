JPG conversion

JPG is widely used for photographs and everyday images. It uses lossy compression and does not support transparent backgrounds. Transparent areas are filled with white when converting to JPG.

PNG conversion

PNG is useful for graphics, screenshots and images that need transparency. PNG files can be larger than JPG files.

WebP conversion

WebP is a modern image format designed for the web. It can offer good image quality with smaller files, depending on the image and compression settings.

Frequently asked questions

Is this image converter free?

Yes. You can use this image converter for free in your browser.

Can I convert images on my phone?

Yes. The page is designed to work on smartphones, tablets and computers. Large images may take longer to process.

Can I convert an image to JPG, PNG or WebP?

Yes, provided your browser can open the selected image and supports the chosen output format.

Will converting an image reduce its quality?

JPG and WebP conversions may use lossy compression. PNG conversion uses a lossless output format, but converting from an already compressed image cannot restore lost detail.

Why does my converted image have a white background?

JPG does not support transparency. Transparent areas are filled with white when converting to JPG.
