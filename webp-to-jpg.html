Why convert WebP to JPG?

WebP is a modern image format often used on websites to reduce file sizes. However, some apps, older programs and online services work better with JPG. Converting WebP to JPG can make an image easier to use in those situations.

Does JPG support transparency?

No. JPG does not support transparent backgrounds. Any transparent areas in the original image are filled with white during conversion.

Will the converted JPG look exactly the same?

JPG uses lossy compression, so the converted image may look slightly different from the original WebP. The output dimensions remain unchanged.

Are my images uploaded?

The conversion is performed locally in your browser. The image is not intentionally sent to our server. Avoid using public or shared devices for sensitive files.
